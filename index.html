Nissan GT-R: The Godzilla

The Nissan GT-R is a legendary sport car that combines exceptional performance with twin turbocharged v6 engine technology. Known worldwide by the nickname "Godzilla", the car continues to set the gold standard in aerodynamic performance and speed engineering.

Feature & Specification

VR38DETT Engine

The 3.8 Liter twin-turbo V6 engine. Hand assembled by Takumi (The Master Craftsmen) produces more than 565 horsepower..

AWD ATTESA E-TS

An intelligent all wheel drive system dynamically distributes power for maximum traction in every turn.

Aerodynamic Design

Engineered to slice through the wind, maximizing downforce while keeping drag remarkably low.
